# /gpw-management — deep dive on management and owners

Investigate: $ARGUMENTS (a company, or "person, company").

"Buying a stock means buying the people." Judge actions, not statements.

## Scope

1. **Capital allocation record** (10 years): acquisitions (and how they turned out), dividends, buybacks, share issues. For each major decision: what was promised vs what happened.
2. **Words vs deeds**: promises from shareholder letters and investor presentations over the last 5 years vs delivery. Quotes with dates.
3. **Skin in the game**: board and management shareholdings, insider transactions (buying or selling?), bonus structure (what do they actually get paid for?).
4. **Treatment of minorities**: history of tender offers, squeeze-outs, related-party deals, dilution.
5. **GPW-specific — the controlling owner**: who really controls the company (State Treasury? founder? fund?), the record of post-election board purges, the risk of non-commercial objectives.

## Sources

ESPI current reports (appointments, dismissals, insider trades), remuneration reports, the KRS registry, press interviews (bankier, parkiet, PB, money.pl), investor forums (leads to verify, never evidence).

## Output

1–5 rating + a "promise vs delivery" table + red-flag list + verdict: would you trust this team with your own money for 10 years (YES/NO/DON'T KNOW). Save to `reports/{TICKER}/{TICKER}-management-{YYYYMMDD}.md`. Write in the user's language.
