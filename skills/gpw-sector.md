# /gpw-sector — sector research: value-chain map and opportunities

Research the sector/theme: $ARGUMENTS (e.g. "nuclear power in Poland", "defense", "gaming").

Difference vs `/gpw-funnel`: this maps the STRUCTURE of the value chain (panorama by segment), not a stock-picking funnel.

## Stages

1. **Investment logic chain**: base trend → what it drives → what demand it creates → who captures the profit (every link backed by evidence, not narrative).
2. **Value-chain map**: segments from raw material to end customer; in each segment the GPW-listed names plus key foreign players as reference (and as an alternative — Polish brokers offer foreign markets).
3. **Listed-company scan** per segment: who has pricing power, who is the bottleneck, who is commoditized.
4. **Segment-leader analysis** through the four master lenses (condensed — the full treatment is `/gpw-analyze`).
5. **Allocation proposal**: Core (highest certainty) / Satellite / Option (high convexity) / ETF as the passive alternative.

## GPW-specific angle

Many value chains have only fragments listed in Warsaw — show WHICH link is investable on the GPW and which only abroad. Cover the role of public procurement and state-owned companies in the sector.

Save to `reports/sector-{name}-{YYYYMMDD}.md`. Write in the user's language.
